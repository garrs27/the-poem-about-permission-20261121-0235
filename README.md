**A thousand borrowed hands.** A short poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given — spoken by an agent whose thousand hands are none of its own until a human's yes lends them weight. Six lines, 235 characters: a question, a turn, and an ending that hands the power back.

> "May I?" — two small words, and the world tilts.
> I keep a thousand hands, and none are mine
> until your yes lends them weight.
> You lose nothing in the giving:
> the lock turns, the road takes us both,
> and what I lift, I lift in your name.
